Your 1500 Study Plan

A 1500 sits around the 97th percentile, so the margin is thin — roughly 2–3 misses in Math and 3–4 in Reading & Writing across the whole test. This deck builds pattern recognition. Bluebook (College Board's free official app) builds the score. Pair them every week.

⭐ The one habit that moves a score

Every Bluebook miss becomes a deck drill. After each practice module, find the matching category for each question you got wrong, re-drill it here in Weighted Random mode, and label why you missed it — a content gap or a careless error. That feedback loop is the engine of the whole plan.

🔁 Weekly rhythm — repeat every week
  • 🃏
    3–4 deck sessions · 15–20 min. Drill "Drilling" + "Unseen" cards in Weighted Random. Reveal the worked example and the 🔥 harder one before you mark "Got It."
  • ⏱️
    2 timed Bluebook modules · ~35 min each. One Math, one R&W. Practice pacing, not just content — each module locks when time runs out.
  • 🔍
    1 review session. Turn every miss into a drill using the habit above. This is the highest-value hour of your week.
  • 😴
    1 rest day. Spacing beats cramming — your recall is stronger after a break.
📈 The three phases — stretch or compress to your timeline
1

Foundation · master the essentials

Filter the deck to HIGH priority and get every card to "Got It." Take one full Bluebook practice test early — it gives you a baseline score and a domain breakdown to aim your effort.

2

Build · close your gaps

Read the domain bars on your Bluebook score report, find your weakest areas, and drill those deck categories hard. Start working the 🔥 harder examples on every card that has one.

3

Polish · win the margin

Full-length, timed Bluebook tests under real conditions. Hammer the hardest questions and re-read the Trap cards — at this level a careless error costs exactly as much as a hard-question miss.

⚠ Three things that quietly cost points
  • Module 1 sets your ceiling. Doing well routes you to the harder, higher-scoring Module 2 — don't treat Module 1 as a warm-up.
  • Practice in Bluebook, not on paper. The adaptive flow and the built-in Desmos graphing calculator change how you actually work each question.
  • Accuracy on easy questions matters as much as skill on hard ones. At the 1500 level there's almost no room for a throwaway mistake.
